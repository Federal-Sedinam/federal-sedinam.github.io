/ about me

I started out as a backend engineer, building APIs and working with Django, Python, and machine learning models. The kind of work that happens behind the scenes but shapes everything the user eventually touches. Over time, I found myself increasingly curious about that other side: the interface people actually see and interact with. That curiosity turned into a deliberate shift toward frontend development.

I'm currently deepening my CSS and JavaScript fundamentals, not just to make things work, but to understand why they work, from layout behavior to responsive design principles. I hold certifications in Product Management and Agile methodologies, which shape how I approach every build: starting with the user's problem, not just the code.

Outside of work, I'm usually building something with my hands too. Often a small robot powered by an Arduino. Whether it's a circuit or a webpage, I like figuring out how things are put together. Oh, I make content too.
